The licence is the one to fix today

California Business & Professions Code §7030.5

California requires a licensed contractor to display their licence number in all forms of advertising, and the Contractors State Licence Board treats a website as advertising. We could not find licence number 1119278 anywhere on nicksroofingservices.com.

Two reasons this matters beyond compliance. First, Californians are told repeatedly to check a contractor's licence before hiring, and a roofer whose number is not on their site looks like a roofer who does not have one. Second, you have a genuinely good licence position — active, with a twenty-five thousand dollar bond — and you are getting no credit for it.

The estimate you cannot actually request

"Free Instant Estimate" appears as a call to action. There is no form behind it — the link goes to a contact page, and the homepage itself contains no form fields at all.

So the word "instant" is doing the opposite of its job. Someone in a hurry clicks it, finds no way to submit anything quickly, and goes back to the search results.

And the number is not tappable

510-815-4451 appears as plain text with no tel: link, so a homeowner on a phone has to memorise it and dial manually.

Separately, your BuildZoom listing carries a different number, (510) 453-3048. Two numbers in circulation splits your call tracking and confuses anyone who finds both.

Why this is a good position, not a bad one

Read this before the priority list

Nothing wrong here is about your work or your standing. Your licence is active and bonded. You have fourteen permitted projects on public record. You carry a wider range of roof systems than most of your competition. Twenty years in Hayward is real.

Every single failure in this audit is a missing piece of plumbing on a website, and all of them are cheap. A form, a clickable phone number, your licence number and your reviews would move you from an F to roughly a C in a single afternoon — and that is before anyone spends a dollar on advertising.

For five to ten extra jobs a month, that plumbing has to exist first. Otherwise advertising sends people to a page with no way to contact you.

Priority order

  1. Put your licence number on every page

    CSLB 1119278 in the footer and on the contact page. It is a legal requirement for advertising in California and it is free credibility you already earned.

  2. Put a real form on the homepage

    Roof type, property type, urgency, address and phone. Then "Free Instant Estimate" becomes true instead of a dead end.

  3. Make the phone number tappable

    A tel: link in the header and footer, and settle on one number across the website, Yelp and BuildZoom.

  4. Get your reviews visible

    Your Yelp listing has photos and history. Ask recent customers for Google reviews and put the rating and count on the site.

  5. Add warranty and insurance terms

    Workmanship warranty length, bond amount and insurance. On a re-roof this is most of what the homeowner is deciding between.

  6. Build the commercial page and the neighbouring city pages

    TPO and PVC deserve their own page for property managers, and San Leandro, Castro Valley, Union City, Fremont and Newark are free ground you are not competing for.
